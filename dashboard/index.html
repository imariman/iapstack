Refund consumption answers

Off

When a customer asks Apple for a refund, Apple may ask this server about the purchase. IAPStack answers within 12 hours only with what it holds: the customer's consent, delivery from a verified purchase, and the declarations below. It never sends a consumption percentage.

Consent is opt-in. IAPStack answers only for customers your backend recorded as consenting, with the application key: PUT /v1/applications/{application_id}/customers/{external_customer_id}/apple-consumption-consent. No record or a recorded no skips the request.
