# Dashboard Page Rules

The dashboard's layout, components and states are documented in [`DESIGN.md`](../../../DESIGN.md). The product direction is in [`MASTER.md`](../MASTER.md).

## Information architecture

1. Persistent project navigation and control-plane identity.
2. Project title, freshness, the primary "New catalog" action, access keys, refresh and sign-out.
3. Store → Verify → Access → Deliver purchase route; a stage that needs attention names its cause.
4. One joined metric panel: applications, customers, products and pending jobs.
5. Revenue and activity: daily verification outcomes beside recognized store revenue and sandbox test value.
6. Applications as dense rows, then the product map and job queues.
7. Evidence tables: recent verifications, customers and notification delivery.
